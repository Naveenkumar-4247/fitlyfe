THE PROJECT

More Than Fitness.

FitLyfe brings fitness tracking, daily routines, goal management and data analytics together in one intelligent platform.

Instead of simply recording your activities, FitLyfe helps you understand your consistency, identify patterns and measure how your daily actions influence your goals.

Every workout, calorie target, water intake, sleep record and daily activity becomes part of your personal progress data.
